General information
Yes. The application via uni-assist is fully digital. Simply upload all of the required documents in your
My assist account
.
The processing time for your documents begins once you have submitted an online application form.
Creating multiple accounts may cause problems with your application.
Please use only one My assist account for your applications.
Please do not register multiple times.
Application
You can easily create the application form yourself in
My assist
:
1. Select your chosen course of study using the search function.
Tip:
Select only your chosen semester and university. You will then see the university’s entire course offer. Then choose your course of study from the results list.
If you are applying for a
preliminary review documentation (VPD)
, the application may show only the academic field "alle Fächer" (= all subjects) in the results list for some universities.
2. Go to your “Selection list” by clicking on the box symbol. Click on “Show additional application-related questions” and answer the questions completely and truthfully.
Note:
The universities supply the application forms, so the questions may vary from university to university.
3. Submit your application by paying the handling fee.
To apply for a
preparatory German language course
or the
DSH
exam, you generally have to first apply for a course of study in
My assist
. Select the course you want to study after the German course or
DSH
exam. To do so, use the search below “
Search courses of study
”:
Select just your chosen semester and university. You will then see the university’s entire course offer.
You found your chosen course of study? Then click on “
Select
.” Fill in the application form completely and truthfully.
Do you see the question “Are you applying for admission to the
DSH
exam / a German language course?” Answer „Yes“ to this question.
You do not see the question? We will then process this application as an application for the
DSH
exam or the German language course automatically.
Two additional notes:
uni-assist is not automatically responsible for all applications for the
DSH
exam or German course at a uni-assist university. Please find out from the
university
directly whether you should apply for their
DSH
exam or German course via uni-assist. If so, also ask whether the university uses a separate application form for their
DSH
exam or German course in My assist.
Please also find out the requirements for attending a German language course or the
DSH
exam from the university.

Please note:
The application via My assist is only possible once your chosen university has activated the applications. Only then you can create an application. Please check with the
university
when they will activate applications in My assist.
This can happen for the following reason:
The application period for your chosen study course may not have begun yet. Please find out from the university when they will activate the online application for your chosen course in My assist.
There may be several reasons for this.
1. Please check that you have answered all the mandatory questions. Only after doing so can you pay for and submit your online application to uni-assist.
2. We use the 3D Secure system for credit card payments. To use this system, you need to be signed up for the 3D Secure system. For more information, please contact your bank.
3. If you enter the wrong data three times, you will be locked out of our payment provider's website for one hour for security reasons. Please try again after one hour has passed.
Upload
If you want to upload documents, please note the following:
You can only upload pdf files.
Each file can be a maximum of 10 MB in size.
A firewall may prevent the uploading of your documents. If so, you will see the error message “Bad Request”. Disable your firewall or try to upload the files from a different computer.
Once we have already processed your documents, you can no longer delete them.
Do you want to update a document that we have already processed? Then simply upload a newer version. We will then take the newest version into consideration when processing your application documents.
You can upload an individual
letter of motivation
or
CV
for each application. If you want to use the same version for all of your applications, one copy of each will be sufficient.
Do you want to upload an individual document for a specific application? Simply name the university and course of study you are applying to, visibly and in bold print, at the very top of your document. We will then be able to assign that document to the right university.
We do not send an automatic confirmation of receipt for documents you upload.
In your My assist account under “My Applications“ / “Documents“ you can see all the documents that you have uploaded.
We can see your uploads and will use them in the processing of your applications.
Troubleshooting
Please do not register again. Simply visit the “
Forgot password
” page.
Enter the email address you used to register in My assist and solve the captcha.
You will then receive an email to reset your password.
After the registration, you received an activation link via email. The activation link is valid for 24 hours.
Check to see whether the email is in your spam folder.
Did you not receive an activation email or has the activation link expired? Then please register again. You will then receive a new activation link from us.
