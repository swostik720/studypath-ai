How it works
Apply in 6 steps
Step 1
Get information in advance
If you would like to study in Germany and uni-assist is responsible for your application, this is the page for you. If you are interested in a
bachelor
or
Master’s course
, you can find out about the requirements here. Then, ask your chosen university about the application criteria for your chosen course of study.
Find out more
If you have all the information you need and uni-assist is responsible for your application,
continue to step 2
.
Step 2
Plan your application
Check how to apply via uni-assist. Plan your application using our checklists and notes on deadlines. In some cases, special rules may apply, for example if you need a preliminary review documentation (
VPD
).
Find out more
Done planning?
Continue with step 3.
Step 3
Assemble your documents
Create scans of all the required documents. Check whether translations are required and, if necessary, have them made by a
Court-certified translator
.
Find out more
Once all your documents are ready,
continue with step 4
.
Step 4
Apply online
Register in My assist. Create an online application form for each chosen course. Upload documents for your applications.
Find out more
Once you have applied online,
continue with step 5
.
Step 5
Pay all fees
Pay the handling fees for all your applications. The processing time begins once we have received your paid online application form.
Find out more
Once you have paid,
continue with step 6
.
Step 6
Send and Track
Track your application’s status in your My assist account.
Find out more
You did it! We can now evaluate your application.
