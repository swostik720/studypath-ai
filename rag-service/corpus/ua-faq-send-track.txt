Uploading documents
You do not need to send documents to uni-assist by post. The application via uni-assist is fully digital. Simply upload all of the required documents in your
My assist account
.

Evaluation phase & status
You will usually receive your evaluation result within
4 to 6 weeks
– counting from the day on which we received your online application form. We evaluate all applications received in order of their arrival.
We inform you by email as soon as the evaluation result is available in your My assist account. You will then find out if your application is complete.
If your application is not complete, we provide you with a list of what is missing. You can
submit missing documents
up until the
application deadline
.

Status, evaluation result and admission
The most important thing is that uni-assist receives your completed application before the
application deadline
expires.
uni-assist has agreed on its own deadlines with the universities for processing applications. This means we can continue to process your application, even after the
deadline
for you to submit your completed application has passed.
Generally, the universities do not start their admission processes until uni-assist has processed all the applications that we received that were complete and submitted within the
application deadline
.
Are you applying for the
preliminary review documentation (VPD)
? If so, you should allow more time for your application, because you generally still have to apply to the university directly using the
VPD
. The agreement with the universities mentioned above does not apply to the
VPD
procedure.
You can check the status of your application in your
My assist account
via the application overview.
You will find the current status directly below your chosen study course. The status will change after we have completely processed your application (or any documents you have submitted later).
To find out what your status means, please read
Status of my application
.
Your application's status stays the same until we have processed your new documents. If we determine that your application now meets all of the university’s criteria, we will then update its status.

No. uni-assist evaluates whether your application meets the criteria for admission. If it does, we forward your application to the university‘s
admission office
.
The university then examines the applications and decides on admission. The university will inform you about whether you will be admitted and how to proceed.

uni-assist is not a public authority and cannot issue official letters that other public authorities recognise.
However: With a positive evaluation result from uni-assist, you can apply for a
student applicant visa
("Studienbewerber-Visum") in the visa section of many German embassies or German consulates.
For more information, please contact your
German embassy, consulate or the foreigners’ registration office ("Ausländerbehörde")
.

Changes
Yes, this is possible if certain conditions are met.
To find out how you can change your chosen study course, please see:
Changing your chosen study course
You can easily change your email address in your
My assist account
via “My Account“ / “Change e-mail address.“
1. Enter your current password. You can now enter your new email address.
2. Confirm your changes by clicking on “Save.“ You will be automatically logged out.
3. We will send an activation link via email to your new email address. Also check your spam folder. Click on the activation link. You can now log in with your new email address.
We will also notify you by an email to your old email address about the change.
You can easily change your password in your
My assist account
via “My Account“ / “Change password.“
1. Enter your current password. You can now enter your new password.
2. Confirm your changes by clicking on “Save.“
3. You will be automatically logged out. You can now log in with your new password.
You can easily correct your personal information yourself – via "My account" in your
My assist account
.
In this menu, first click on "Change applicant information," then select "Edit applicant information" in the "Personal information" tab. You can now edit your information.
Confirm your changes by checking the box next to the mandatory field and enter your password. To finish, click on "Save."
